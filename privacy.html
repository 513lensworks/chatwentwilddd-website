Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatWentWilddd handles information in connection with its gaming highlight and social media publishing tools.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process:

  • Social media account identifiers and basic profile information returned by authorized platform APIs.
  • Authorization tokens needed to access connected accounts.
  • Video files, captions, descriptions, and publishing metadata.
  • Upload results, platform video identifiers, and error logs.
  • Information voluntarily provided in support emails.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, prepare and publish authorized videos, monitor upload results, troubleshoot failures, and respond to inquiries.

4. Platform Integrations

ChatWentWilddd may connect to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and other supported platforms through their authorized APIs. Data accessed through these integrations is intended to support the publishing functions requested by the user.

Each platform independently processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Storage and Retention

The intended video-processing workflow retains temporary video files only as needed for processing and publishing. Successfully published local video files are intended to be deleted approximately 24 hours after all required uploads are confirmed.

Failed uploads may require longer retention for retries. Upload records, identifiers, and operational logs may be retained for troubleshooting and duplicate prevention. Retention practices will be reviewed as the service develops.

6. Data Security

We intend to use reasonable safeguards to protect authorization credentials and application data, including limiting access to authorized operators and avoiding publication of private credentials in public repositories. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

Information needed to publish content is transmitted to the social media platforms selected by the user. Hosting providers may process data as necessary to operate the application. We do not intend to sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

8. Account Disconnection and Deletion Requests

Users may revoke connected application permissions through the relevant social media platform's account settings. To request deletion of information held by ChatWentWilddd, email chatwentwild23@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Revoking access does not automatically remove videos already published to third-party platforms.

9. Children's Privacy

ChatWentWilddd is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13 through the application.

10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as application features and data practices change. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: chatwentwild23@gmail.com .
