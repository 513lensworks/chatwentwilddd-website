When Chat Goes Wilddd.

ChatWentWilddd is a gaming highlight project focused on discovering memorable livestream moments and preparing engaging short-form videos for social media.

What We're Building

Highlight Discovery

Identify promising moments from authorized gaming livestreams using viewer and chat activity.

Vertical Video Editing

Prepare short clips with vertical formatting and readable captions for mobile viewing.

Content Publishing

Develop tools to manage and publish authorized clips across supported social media platforms.

Responsible Content

ChatWentWilddd is intended for content that its users own or have permission to use. Publishing features depend on the permissions and policies of each platform.
